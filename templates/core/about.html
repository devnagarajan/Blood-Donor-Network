About RedFusion

Our Mission

To create a seamless connection between blood donors, hospitals, and NGOs to ensure that no one suffers due to lack of blood availability.

Our Vision

A world where every patient in need of blood transfusion receives it on time, and where blood donation becomes a regular part of social responsibility.

How the Platform Works

👥

Donors

Register with your blood group and location. Receive notifications when your blood type is needed nearby.

🏥

Hospitals

Create blood requests with patient details. Our system automatically finds matching donors and notifies them.

🤝

NGOs

Coordinate between donors and hospitals. Organize blood donation camps and manage community outreach.

Why Blood Donation Matters

  • ✓ Every 2 seconds someone needs blood
  • ✓ One donation can save up to 3 lives
  • ✓ Blood cannot be manufactured - it can only come from donors
  • ✓ Only 10% of eligible people donate blood
  • ✓ Regular donation helps maintain healthy iron levels
