Health Benefits for Donors

While helping save lives, blood donation also offers significant health benefits to you as the donor!

❤️ Reduces Heart Disease Risk

Regular donors have 88% lower risk of heart attacks!

🩸 New Blood Cells

Stimulates production of fresh blood cells.

⚖️ Burns Calories

One donation burns approximately 650 calories!

🔬 Free Health Check

Get free screening every time you donate.

Free Health Screening Includes:

  • Blood pressure measurement
  • Hemoglobin level testing
  • Screening for infectious diseases (HIV, Hepatitis B & C, Syphilis)
  • Blood type identification
💡 Bonus Benefit

Knowing you've helped save lives releases endorphins - the "feel-good" hormones!
