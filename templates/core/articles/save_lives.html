One donation can save up to three lives

Why Your Donation Matters

Every blood donation can save up to three lives. When you donate blood, you're giving hope and life to someone in desperate need. Blood is essential for emergencies, surgeries, cancer treatments, and chronic illnesses.

💡 Did You Know?

Someone needs blood every 2 seconds. Your donation could be the difference between life and death.

Who Benefits From Your Donation?

🚑 Accident Victims

Trauma patients often require multiple blood transfusions immediately.

🏥 Surgery Patients

Complex surgeries require blood to be available during operations.

💉 Cancer Patients

Chemotherapy patients often need regular blood transfusions.

🩺 Chronic Illness

People with blood disorders need regular transfusions to stay healthy.

Impact of Your Single Donation

One blood donation (450ml) can be separated into:

  • Red Blood Cells: For trauma victims and surgical patients
  • Platelets: For cancer patients and clotting disorders
  • Plasma: For burn victims and liver disease patients
